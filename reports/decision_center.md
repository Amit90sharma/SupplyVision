
# 📊 SupplyVision – Decision Center

## Executive Summary

The SupplyVision Retail Data Warehouse was analyzed using SQL and Power BI to evaluate sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, supplier contribution, product returns, and operational efficiency.

Based on the analysis, the following business actions are recommended.

---

# Business Recommendations

| Priority  | Business Area        | Observation                                                                                             | Recommendation                                                                                                     | Expected Business Impact                                                  |
| --------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔴 High   | Customer Management  | A small group of customers generates a significant share of revenue (Customer Lifetime Value analysis). | Launch a loyalty and rewards program for high-value customers to improve retention and repeat purchases.           | Higher customer retention and increased lifetime revenue.                 |
| 🔴 High   | Product Quality      | Returned products contribute to refund costs and revenue leakage.                                       | Investigate products with the highest return rates and collaborate with suppliers to improve quality.              | Lower refund expenses and improved customer satisfaction.                 |
| 🔴 High   | Store Performance    | Revenue varies across retail stores.                                                                    | Benchmark top-performing stores and replicate successful operational practices across lower-performing locations.  | Improved sales consistency across the retail network.                     |
| 🟠 Medium | Inventory Management | Product categories contribute differently to total revenue.                                             | Allocate inventory based on category performance and prioritize high-demand categories.                            | Reduced stock shortages and improved inventory turnover.                  |
| 🟠 Medium | Supplier Management  | Supplier contribution differs across the product portfolio.                                             | Strengthen relationships with high-performing suppliers and periodically review low-performing supplier contracts. | Better procurement efficiency and product availability.                   |
| 🟠 Medium | Promotions           | Promotion performance varies between campaigns.                                                         | Continue successful promotional campaigns and redesign underperforming promotions using sales insights.            | Higher marketing ROI and improved campaign effectiveness.                 |
| 🟢 Low    | Sales Monitoring     | Revenue trends fluctuate across months and quarters.                                                    | Implement automated monthly performance reviews using the Power BI dashboard.                                      | Faster identification of sales trends and improved decision-making.       |
| 🟢 Low    | Executive Reporting  | Business metrics are distributed across multiple operational systems.                                   | Use the SupplyVision dashboard as a centralized reporting solution for management.                                 | Reduced reporting time and improved visibility into business performance. |

---

# Strategic Insights

### Customer Analytics

* Focus retention efforts on high-value customers identified through Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) analysis.
* Introduce personalized offers and loyalty incentives.

---

### Sales Performance

* Continuously monitor store performance using revenue and order metrics.
* Investigate operational differences between top and lower-performing stores.

---

### Inventory Optimization

* Prioritize procurement for high-performing product categories.
* Review slow-moving inventory periodically.

---

### Supplier Evaluation

* Monitor supplier performance based on revenue contribution.
* Review supplier contracts using data-driven performance metrics.

---

### Return Management

* Track return rates at the product level.
* Reduce refund costs through quality improvement initiatives.

---

# Expected Business Outcomes

* Improved customer retention
* Better inventory allocation
* Reduced product returns
* Increased operational efficiency
* Data-driven supplier management
* Centralized executive reporting
* Faster business decision-making

---

# Conclusion

SupplyVision transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business intelligence by combining ETL pipelines, data quality validation, relational data modeling, SQL analytics, and interactive Power BI dashboards. The Decision Center demonstrates how analytical insights can be translated into practical business recommendations that support strategic and operational decision-making.
